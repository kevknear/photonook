## Deleting photos

### Where do deleted photos go?

To **Recently Deleted** in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days. Nothing
PhotoNook does is permanent. To get something back: Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted
→ Select → Recover.

### Why does iOS ask me to confirm every deletion?

That confirmation comes from iOS, not from PhotoNook, and no app can turn it off. It's a
deliberate protection so no app can empty your library without you agreeing.

What PhotoNook *can* do is ask fewer times, by deleting photos in groups instead of one
by one. In **Settings → Deletion mode** you have three options:

- **End of session** (default) — nothing is deleted until you review the tray, then it
  all goes in one confirmation.
- **In batches** — deletes automatically every N photos, one confirmation per batch.
- **Every swipe** — deletes immediately, one confirmation per photo.

If you're seeing a lot of confirmations, you're probably on *Every swipe*.

### I discarded a photo by mistake

If you haven't confirmed the deletion yet, open the **To delete** tab and tap the photo
to unmark it, or press and hold it and choose *Send back to the deck*.

If it's already deleted, it's in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

## Your progress

### Does PhotoNook remember what I already reviewed?

Yes. It records what you decided about each photo, so you can clean up over several
sittings without going through the same photos twice. Reviewed photos appear dimmed in
the gallery with a heart or a trash icon.

### How do I start over?

**Settings → Progress → Clear review history.** Every photo shows up as unreviewed
again. It doesn't delete or restore any photos — it only forgets your decisions.

To redo just one section, use *Go through this section again* on the summary screen.

## iCloud

### Some photos have a cloud badge. What does it mean?

The original of that photo lives in iCloud rather than on your iPhone. That's normal if
you use **Optimize iPhone Storage**: iOS keeps small previews on the device and the
full-size originals in iCloud.

### Will PhotoNook use my cellular data?

By default, no. **Settings → Photos stored in iCloud** is set to **Wi-Fi only**, so on
cellular you'll see the local preview and a *Load full quality* button if you want that
specific photo. You can also set it to **Never**, which downloads nothing ever, or
**Always** if you don't mind the data.

Browsing the gallery grid never downloads anything, on any setting.

## Photo access

### PhotoNook says it can't see my photos

It needs read and write access. Read-only isn't enough, because it has to be able to
delete what you discard. Go to **Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoNook**
and choose **Full Access**.

### I picked "Limited Access" and something looks off

With limited access iOS only shows PhotoNook the photos you selected, so the counts and
sections will reflect just those. Switch to Full Access in iOS Settings to see your
whole library.
