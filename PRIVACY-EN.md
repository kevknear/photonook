# PhotoNook Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 31, 2026

*[Versión en español](../privacidad/)*

## Summary

PhotoNook does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing happens on your
device. There are no servers, accounts, analytics, ads, or trackers.

## Access to your photos

PhotoNook requests read and write access to your photo library so it can show you your images and
delete the ones you choose to discard. That access is used for nothing else:

- **Your photos never leave your device.** They are not uploaded, copied, or shared with anyone.
- **Your photos are never transmitted over the network.** The app makes no internet requests.
- Deletion goes through Apple's Photos framework, which moves items to **Recently Deleted**, where
  they stay for 30 days and can be restored from the Photos app.
- You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Without it, the app
  cannot function.

## Data stored on your device

The only information PhotoNook saves is your appearance preference (System, Light, or Dark), stored
locally using `UserDefaults`. It is removed when you delete the app.

## Data collected

None. Specifically, PhotoNook does **not** collect:

- Contact details, identifiers, or account information
- Location
- Usage data, analytics, or crash reports
- Advertising identifiers
- The contents of your photos or their metadata

## Third parties

PhotoNook contains no third-party SDKs, analytics services, or ad networks.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, regardless
of age.
